## Editorial workflow

1. Review current primary sources from model providers, standards bodies, and original research.
2. Select one development with a clear operational consequence for organisations.
3. Draft a practical Nimrod Digitals point of view without inventing results or client evidence.
4. Verify every time-sensitive or quantitative claim against its primary source.
5. Submit the title, excerpt, article, and sources for approval.
6. After approval, publish in WordPress, rebuild the static frontend, deploy, verify, update the sitemap, and request indexing.

## Content pillars

- Agentic operating models
- Human judgment and decision design
- Enterprise AI governance and safeguards
- Service design for AI-enabled work
- Practical adoption signals from frontier research
