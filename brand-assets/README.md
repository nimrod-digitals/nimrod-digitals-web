# Nimrod Digitals Brand Assets — Review Draft

This review set follows the supplied references:

- Logo geometry: interlocking open-form `N` from the Human Systems reference.
- Palette: Ink Black `#0B0B0D`, Electric Blue `#0A57FF`, Navy Blue `#08215C`, Bone White `#F6F3EE`, Stone Grey `#8F9398`.
- Typography: Inter Bold for wordmarks/display use and Inter Regular for secondary/body use.

## Typography decision

Inter Bold was approved as the permanent display and wordmark replacement after review. Inter is also used at regular and medium weights for secondary and body typography, keeping the system consistent and free of commercial font dependencies.

## Included review assets

- Light, dark, and monochrome symbol-only SVG logos.
- Light and dark horizontal and stacked logo lockups.
- Light and dark 1080×1080 profile images for Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, X, and other social platforms.
- Light/dark 1500×500 social banners.
- 2560×1440 YouTube channel banner with content centered in the universal safe region.
- 1200×630 social/open-graph card.
- Light/dark 1920×1080 presentation-deck covers.
- A consolidated browser preview in `preview/index.html`.
- Native-dimension PNG review exports in `png/` matching each SVG source.

The PNGs are native-dimension exports regenerated from the final Inter-based SVG sources.
